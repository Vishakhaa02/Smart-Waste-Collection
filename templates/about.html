About CircuitBin

A small platform built to make waste collection easier to request, track, and manage.

The problem

Many residents aren't sure how to sort or dispose of different kinds of waste, and don't have a straightforward way to request a pickup. On the other side, collection teams often coordinate requests informally — over calls or messages — with no shared record of what's pending, scheduled, or completed.

The solution

CircuitBin gives residents a short form to describe their waste and pickup details, guided by a plain-language category guide, and a tracking code so they can check status without calling anyone. Collection staff get a dashboard to search, filter, and update every request, see collection statistics at a glance, export data, and post announcements when schedules change.
